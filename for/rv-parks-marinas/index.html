Questions RV parks, marinas and lake rentals ask

Does Guest Texts work with my reservation software?

We set it up from your reservation software if it can send new bookings over, or from a simple booking form if it can't. We'll check which one fits during setup.

Yes. The day-before text can include directions, the gate code, Wi-Fi and your rules, so guests arrive with everything they need.

No. You keep your number. King Connect only steps in when a call goes unanswered.
