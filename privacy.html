Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

King Connect ("we," "us") is a local business run by Jordan King in the Cedar Creek Lake area of Texas. We set up missed-call text back, review requests and related marketing tools for local businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through kingconnecttx.com and our text messaging, and how we use it.

The short version: We use your information only to reply to you and provide our services. We never sell it. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, business name, type of business, phone number, and anything else you choose to give us (email, how you heard about us, a message).
  • Calls, texts and emails: your phone number or email address and what you send us.
  • Booking a call: the name, email and time you choose when you book through our Google Calendar booking page.
  • Website visits: basic usage information such as pages viewed, device type and general location, collected with Google Analytics. This does not identify you by name.

How we use it

  • To answer your request, schedule your free check and talk with you about our services.
  • To send text messages you've agreed to receive (see "Text messaging" below).
  • To provide and support the services our customers sign up for.
  • To understand how our website is used so we can improve it.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you give us your phone number and agree to receive texts, we may text you about your request, your appointment and our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving messages at any time, or HELP for help.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Agreeing to receive texts is never a condition of buying anything.

Full messaging terms are in our Terms of Service.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers we use to run the business, and only as needed for them to do that work. These include Google (forms, email, calendar and analytics) and the software platform we use to send and receive texts and calls. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Our customers' customers

When we send texts for a business that uses King Connect (for example, a review request after a job), we do it on that business's behalf. That business decides who gets messages, and its own privacy policy applies to its customers. Anyone can reply STOP to any message to opt out.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as we need it to work with you or as the law requires, then delete it. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to opt out of texts.
  • Email or call us to see, correct or delete the information we have about you.
  • You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
