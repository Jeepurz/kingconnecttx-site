Questions

Straight answers

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Your customers keep calling the same number. We forward unanswered calls through our system so the text-back comes from your business. Office landlines and business phone lines can usually text from that same number. If your business number is your cell phone, texts come from a local King Connect number signed with your business name.

Will I have to learn new software?

No. Everything runs in the background. If you want to read and answer texts, there's a simple app for your phone, but you don't have to use it.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month. If it isn't paying for itself, cancel any time. And if you're not happy in your first month, you get that month back.

What exactly are Guest Texts?

An add-on for businesses where people book a stay: RV parks, marinas, cabins and lake-house rentals. Guests automatically get a booking confirmation, arrival info with directions and gate codes the day before, a thank-you with a review link after checkout, and a deal to come back in the off-season.

Can you get rid of bad reviews?

No one honest can. What we do is get your happy customers to post, so one bad review doesn't define you, and we help you reply to tough reviews professionally.

Are texting costs extra?

Normal texting volume for a local business is included in every plan. If you send thousands of messages a month, we'll talk about it first.
